Research

Undergraduate thesis · 2026–Present · Ongoing

A Structure-Aware LLM-Based Framework for Graph Node Labeling

My undergraduate research asks whether authentic local graph evidence helps language models label nodes, and how those labels affect a downstream GCN. GraphScope presents the recorded Cora comparison between B1 (target text) and Narrative v0 (text plus local evidence), using the same underlying LLM.

Historical annotations and matched GCN outcomes are kept separate from reconstructed SARA verification and arbitration methods. The public snapshot does not establish a SARA improvement.

GraphScope repository & documentation · Original workflow capture

Graph neighborhoodsLLMs / GNNsActive selectionConfidence filtering

Current research directions

  1. Graph context in prompts

    Compare prompts incorporating real one- and two-hop graph evidence on Cora and CiteSeer, alongside semantic node information.

  2. Choosing nodes to label

    Inspect frozen FeatProp targets, separate filtering and loss conditions, and paired historical GCN outcomes in the research atlas.

  3. Understanding annotation failures

    Examine failed and conflicting LLM annotations, confidence limitations, and evaluation leakage. An output-inspection interface is in progress.

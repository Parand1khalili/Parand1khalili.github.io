04 Research / Undergraduate thesis

Research

Ongoing work at Amirkabir University of Technology on label-efficient graph node classification.

Undergraduate thesis · 2026–Present · Ongoing

A Structure-Aware LLM-Based Framework for Graph Node Labeling

Developing a label-efficient node classification framework that combines LLM-generated annotations with graph neural networks on text-attributed graphs, in Dr. Mostafa Haghir Chehreghani's lab.

This work is ongoing. The directions below describe what I am investigating; they are not published findings or completed results.

Graph neighborhoodsLLMs / GNNsActive selectionConfidence filtering

Current research directions

  1. 01

    Bring structure into the prompt

    Explore 1-hop and 2-hop neighbors and random walks as context for LLM-based graph node labeling.

  2. 02

    Choose which nodes to label

    Investigate active node selection to make more effective use of limited annotation resources.

  3. 03

    Learn with confidence

    Study confidence-based filtering of generated annotations and their use in training graph neural networks.

Research

Ongoing work at Amirkabir University of Technology on label-efficient graph node classification.

Undergraduate thesis · 2026–Present · Ongoing

A Structure-Aware LLM-Based Framework for Graph Node Labeling

Implemented and extended an LLM-to-GNN workflow inspired by published label-free methods, under the supervision of Dr. Mostafa Haghir Chehreghani. Controlled prompt comparisons on Cora and CiteSeer examine pseudo-label accuracy, confidence, and downstream GCN behavior.

This work is ongoing. The directions below describe what I am investigating; they are not published findings or completed results.

Graph neighborhoodsLLMs / GNNsActive selectionConfidence filtering

Current research directions

  1. Graph context in prompts

    Compare prompts incorporating real one- and two-hop graph evidence on Cora and CiteSeer, alongside semantic node information.

  2. Choosing nodes to label

    Prepare a matched FeatProp-based evaluation to study node selection and annotation efficiency under comparable conditions.

  3. Understanding annotation failures

    Examine failed and conflicting LLM annotations, confidence limitations, and evaluation leakage. An output-inspection interface is in progress.
